Explainable Machine Learning Architecture

End-to-end data processing workflow from raw text to probabilistic classification & feature attribution

01

NLP Sanitization

Removes HTML, strips punctuation noise, normalizes URLs & currencies into canonical tokens, and removes English stopwords.

02

TF-IDF Feature Space

Projects n-grams (1, 2) into a 4,000-dimensional sparse feature space with sublinear term frequency scaling.

03

Calibrated Inference

Classifies vectors using Platt-scaled Linear SVM boundaries to yield true probabilities and confidence levels.

04

Explainable AI (XAI)

Decomposes decision value into word attributions (w_i · x_i) to visually highlight suspicious words and legitimate anchors.
